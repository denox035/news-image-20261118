# The World in One Day — 18 November 2026

A make.fun news-image entry: one picture summing up the world news of
Wednesday 18 November 2026. The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard
library only) deterministically copies the bundled `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`.

## The day's stories in the image

1. **Gulf war escalates** — The US struck an Iranian missile site on
   Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran answered with strikes on
   US targets in Kuwait (Ali Al Salem Air Base) and Bahrain.
   (Reuters; Just Security Early Edition, 18 Nov 2026)
2. **Kerch Bridge hit** — Ukraine struck the bridge to Crimea for a
   second day; part of its road span collapsed into the strait.
   (Associated Press; Reuters)
3. **Xi, Putin and Trump to meet** — Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met
   in Shenzhen, a day before a first trilateral summit with Donald Trump.
   (AFP; Associated Press; BBC)
4. **Peltola wins Alaska** — Democrat Mary Peltola defeated Republican
   incumbent Dan Sullivan, giving Democrats a 56–44 Senate majority.
   (Associated Press)
5. **Ebola passes 11,000 cases** — The DR Congo outbreak more than
   doubled in three months, with over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths.
   (United Nations, via Reuters)
6. **Voyager 1: one light-day** — Voyager 1 reached one light-day from
   Earth, about 16 billion miles, a first for any human-made object.
   (NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory)
